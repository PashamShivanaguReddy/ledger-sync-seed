# INC-2026-09-11 — a customer's ledger shows a spend they did not make

**Raised:** 11 Sep 2026, 03:14 IST
**Severity:** S2 — wrong financial data shown to a user
**Status:** RESOLVED

---

## What happened

Overnight support picked up a complaint from a user on account ending **4821.

> "Your app says I spent ₹92,213.10 on a water can. I paid ₹5. Fix this, I am
> not able to trust anything else on this screen now."

The Track screen does show a debit of **₹92,213.10** against merchant
**UPI/WATER CAN**. The user is right: the bank SMS behind it says ₹5.

## The alert that fired

```
2026-09-11T03:14:06+05:30  ALERT  ledger.balance.divergence
  account=**4821
  ledger_derived_balance=-1213003.85
  bank_stated_balance=41126.34
  divergence=-1254130.19
  window=last_45d
```

## What we have

`incident/app.log` — the ingest log for the affected account, including the

The row is also still sitting in the legacy store. You will find it in
`db/migration/V2__seed.sql`.

## Resolution & Post-Mortem

### 1. Reproduction
- Executed `Amounts.first("Rs.5 debited from a/c **4821 on 04-07-26 at 07:19 to UPI/WATER CAN. Avl Bal: Rs.92,213.10.")`.
- Result produced: `92213.10` instead of `5.00`.
- Verified via failing test `AmountsTest.readsIntegerRupeesBeforeBalance()`.

### 2. Root Cause
- **File:** `src/main/java/in/simplifymoney/ledgersync/parse/Amounts.java`, Lines 17–18.
- **Defect:** `Pattern.compile("(?:Rs\\.?|INR)\\s*([0-9,]+\\.[0-9]{2})")` strictly required two decimal digits (`\\.[0-9]{2}`).
- When encountering integer amounts (such as `Rs.5`), the pattern failed to match the transaction amount and matched the next decimal currency token: `Rs.92,213.10` (the Available Balance).

### 3. Blast Radius
- **Decision Rule:** A message is affected if:
  1. Its transaction amount is an integer rupee value without decimals (`Rs.5`, `INR 18,000`, `INR 4,200`), AND
  2. It is followed by an available balance or credit limit containing two decimals (`Avl Bal: Rs.92,213.10`).
  *(If an integer transaction has no subsequent decimal balance, `Amounts.first()` returns `null` and drops the transaction).*
- **Impact in `fixtures/corpus-a.jsonl`:**
  - **44 messages** contain integer amounts.
  - **38 messages** suffered balance corruption (transaction amount replaced by the customer's account balance).
  - **2 transactions** were dropped completely (`m-00002-69e4cd` with `INR 45,000` and `m-00043-4add25` with `Rs.2,750`).
  - **4 messages** were non-transaction loan promotions (`Rs.5,00,000`).

### 4. Why Existing Tests Were Green
- `AmountsTest.java` only tested decimal values (`2499.50`, `333.33`, `45000.00`). No test ever tested integer amounts without decimal points.

### 5. Five Lines for Incident Channel
```text
1. What broke: Regex in Amounts.java required two decimal places, skipping integer amounts like Rs.5 and extracting the subsequent balance (Rs.92,213.10) as the transaction amount.
2. How found: Customer complaint on account **4821 and automated balance divergence alert (divergence=-1,254,130.19).
3. Who affected: 38 transaction messages in batch b-20260910-2214 were corrupted with balance figures, and 2 transactions were dropped.
4. Fix applied: Updated Amounts.java regex to accept integer and decimal currency amounts, standardizing to 2 decimal places with scale 2, and isolated balance patterns.
5. Prevention: Added regression unit tests for integer rupee amounts and enabled balance reconciliation checkpointing in the ingestion pipeline.
```
